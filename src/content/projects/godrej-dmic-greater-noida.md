Godrej Properties is planning a residential project of about 23 acres inside the DMIC (Delhi–Mumbai Industrial Corridor) integrated township in Greater Noida. The township is planned with offices, a sports complex and an 18-hole golf course, and sits close to NH-91, the Eastern Peripheral Expressway and the planned Boraki transport hub.

Homes range from 1 BHK to 3 BHK with a study or utility room, in "Grande" and "Luxe" versions.

### Before you pay an EOI

The project is taking expressions of interest (EOI). The marketing partner lists these EOI amounts:

| Home | EOI amount |
| --- | --- |
| 1 BHK | ₹2 Lakh |
| 2 BHK | ₹3 Lakh |
| 3 BHK | ₹4 Lakh |
| 3 BHK + Study or 3 BHK + U | ₹5 Lakh |

The project's UP RERA registration isn't confirmed yet. We'll check the registration on up-rera.in, and get the refund terms for the EOI in writing, before we recommend paying anything.
