Leafsong Retreat is a new Hero Homes project of three high-rise towers in Sector Mu, Greater Noida, next to Omicron II and III. Every home is a 3 BHK, from 1,656 to 2,289 sq. ft. of saleable area. "2T" and "3T" are the number of toilets; "SR" is a servant room.

The site is planned around three landscape zones (a celebration lawn, a sports area and a quiet wellness garden) over a podium with a lap pool. Parking is in the basement, so the ground level stays car-free.

### Included in the price

- ERV fresh-air system in homes and the clubhouse
- Modular kitchen with hob, chimney, RO and geyser
- Engineered marble flooring
- Split air conditioners (Daikin or equivalent)
- False ceiling and lights in the living room
- Master bathroom with vanity, shower cubicle, mirror and geyser

### Launch payment plan

Available during the launch period only. The expression of interest (EOI) is ₹10 Lakh.

| Instalment | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Booking amount, less EOI | 10% |
| Within 60 days of booking | 10% |
| Within 120 days of booking | 5% |
| Ground floor complete, or 18 months* | 12.5% |
| 10th floor complete, or 25 months* | 10% |
| 18th floor complete, or 32 months* | 10% |
| Superstructure complete | 12.5% |
| Flooring begins (excluding wooden) | 15% |
| Application for OC | 10% |
| Offer of possession | 5% |

\*Whichever is later. That works out to 25% in the first year, 12.5% in the second, 20% in the third and 42.5% in the fourth. Lease rent is collected at offer of possession.
