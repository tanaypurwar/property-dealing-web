Nirala Diadem is a mixed-use building by Nirala World on a corner plot in Sector 10, Greater Noida West, open on three sides with about 130 metres of road frontage. The lower floors hold retail shops, restaurants, a banquet hall, a family entertainment zone and a multiplex cinema, with studio apartments in the tower above.

The location is the main draw. Sector 10 is surrounded by large housing societies: the developer's catchment study counts over 1.35 lakh residents within 3 km today, and more than 3 lakh once nearby projects are occupied. A metro station is proposed in Sector 10, within walking distance, on the planned line from Ghaziabad to Noida International Airport.

The studio apartments are aimed at working professionals and at investors who want rental income. Nirala World has been building in Noida and Greater Noida since 1996; its earlier projects include Nirala Estate, Trio and Gateway.
