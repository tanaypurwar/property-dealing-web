Sikka Kaamya Greens is a residential project in Sector 10, Greater Noida West, on land allotted by the Greater Noida Authority. It has ten apartment types, from a 980 sq. ft. 2 BHK to a 2,120 sq. ft. 4 BHK, arranged around a central park with a clubhouse, pool and a shopping complex.

The project is backed by the SWAMIH Investment Fund, the government-sponsored fund managed by SBICAP Ventures that finances the completion of stalled housing projects, and Tata Projects is the construction partner. According to the developer's October 2026 ads, Phase 1 has applied for its occupancy certificate. Until the OC is issued, homes can't be legally handed over, so ask us to check its status before you book.

About three-quarters of the plot is open space, and the site borders the designated green belt on two sides. The architects, RTA, designed the towers so that most flats face the greens.

### Carpet and saleable area

Carpet area is the space inside your walls, and it's what RERA requires builders to sell by. The difference between the two figures is your share of walls, balconies and common areas.
