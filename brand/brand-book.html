Part A

Brand strategy

Purpose
To make buying property in NCR something you can check, instead of something you have to take on faith.
Vision
To be the name NCR buyers check with before they sign anything.
Mission
List only verified projects, show the full all-inclusive price before the site visit, and stay with every buyer until registry.

Positioning statement

For upgraders, investors and NRIs buying in Noida and Greater Noida who are tired of half-quotes and promises they can't verify, Propertydealing.in is the RERA-certified property advisor that shows the full, all-inclusive price upfront and stays with you until registry, because every project we list is personally verified and carries its RERA number.

Why this position. The client mostly loses deals to local brokers, often after a portal lead has been passed along. Portals can't compete on personal verification and staying with the buyer, and local brokers rarely compete on transparency. So we take the ground neither of them holds: a local advisor who is accountable in writing.

Brand pillars

Pillar 1

Verified before it's listed

Our team visits the site, checks the RERA registration and confirms the developer's approvals before a project reaches the website. If we haven't checked it, we don't show it.

Pillar 2

The full price, first

An all-inclusive price breakup (BSP, PLC, IFMS, parking, club, GST, stamp duty and registry) comes before the site visit. The number you see is the number you pay. This answers the "hidden charges" fear directly.

Pillar 3

One advisor, till registry

The person who shows you the project is the person who helps with the loan, the paperwork and registry. A dedicated, detail-oriented team, with no hand-offs to strangers.

Pillar 4

Your time, your terms

No cold-call storms. You pick the call time, or you talk on WhatsApp. We never pass your number to other brokers. This answers the "spam calls" fear directly.

Part B

Verbal identity

Personality and archetype

Primary archetype: the Sage. A knowledgeable guide who explains, shows evidence and lets the buyer decide. Secondary: the Caregiver. This brings the warmth of staying with a family until registry.

Why. NCR property marketing is full of Rulers ("iconic", "majestic") and Magicians ("dream home"). A Sage brand stands out at once, and it fits the client's words: transparent, grounded, sharp.

Part C

Visual identity

The existing logo has two assets worth keeping: the orange sun with a black ring set over it, and the confident monoline drawing. Its generic houses and trees are what we leave behind.

Three logo concepts

Recommended

1 · Clear Sun

Idea: the ring and sun from the old logo, redrawn so the ring becomes the bowl of a lowercase p.
Symbolism: the closed ring is the complete picture, with price, RERA and possession and no gaps. The sun rising behind it is a new beginning. A sun rising in the east is also a vastu-positive cue many NCR buyers notice.
Type: Manrope ExtraBold, outlined; ".in" in Manrope Bold at 62%.
Colour: Ink ring, Sunrise disc, with a paper gap between them so it also works in one colour.
Why it suits NCR: it carries over equity the client already owns, it is two shapes so it holds at 16px, and it is the only orange-and-black "p" in a market full of navy roofs.

2 · Plot & Tick

Idea: a square plot boundary whose corner breaks open into a check mark.
Symbolism: verified inventory. The land is checked before it is sold.
Type: Manrope Bold with tight tracking.
Colour: Ink boundary, Sunrise tick.
Trade-off: very clear, but check marks are common in fintech and verification brands, and it drops the sun the client already owns.

3 · Horizon Line

Idea: one continuous line that draws a high-rise skyline and finishes in a "d" loop, under a small sun.
Symbolism: the NCR skyline, and one unbroken process from site visit to registry.
Type: Manrope SemiBold, open tracking.
Colour: Ink line, Sunrise sun.
Trade-off: closest to the original artwork, but fine lines blur at 32px and skylines are the most common real estate cliché after roofs.

Why Clear Sun. It is the only concept that meets all three tests in the brief. It is distinctive with no roof or house, it reads at 32px and on a 20-foot hoarding, and its meaning (the full picture) is the brand promise itself.

Clear space

2x

x = the width of the p's stem, about 1/8 of the lockup's height. Keep at least 2x clear on every side. No text, edge or graphic may enter it.

Minimum and large-format sizes

  • Below 24 px, use only favicon.svg. It is a solid tile, so it shows on light and dark browser tabs.
  • Hoardings: the horizontal logo should be at least 1/4 of the board's width. On a 20 ft board that makes it 5 ft wide, with a cap height of about 110 mm, which is legible from about 40 m.
  • Always print from the SVG (or a PDF exported from it), never from a PNG.

Logo don'ts

Don't stretch, squash or change proportions.

Don't recolour the sun. It is always Sunrise, or the single colour of a mono version.

Don't rotate or tilt the logo.

Don't place it on busy photos. Use a Paper or Ink plate.

No shadows, glows, bevels or gradients.

Property Dealing

Don't retype the name in another font. Always use the artwork.

Don't add roofs, keys or houses to the mark.

Don't fade it or use it as a watermark below 100%.

Imagery, icons and graphic devices

Photography

  • Real site photos, shot at eye level in morning light. The warm sunrise tone links back to the mark.
  • Show the true construction stage, with a date stamp ("Photographed Oct 2026").
  • Label every render "Artist's impression".
  • Show real people: our advisors on site, families at handover. No stock couples holding keys.
  • Never use heavy HDR, fake skies or drone shots that hide the neighbourhood.

Icons

  • A 2 px monoline outline on a 24 px grid with rounded joins, the drawing style kept from the old logo.
  • Ink by default, with Sunrise reserved for one active state.
  • Lucide icons match this style, so use them as the base set instead of drawing custom icons.

Graphic devices

  • The skyline rule: one continuous monoline of NCR towers, used as a footer edge, a section divider or a board base.
  • The verification ring: a small Sunrise ring marks verified facts (RERA ✓, price ✓). It is a fragment of the logo used as a checkmark.
  • The price breakup: itemised price tables are a visual signature, set in tabular figures and never hidden.
